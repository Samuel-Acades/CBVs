How it's used

The recommendation engine cross-checks each CBV against these files:
  • Received last month? → held back (already got a bundle)
  • Never received → priority boost
  • Received a while ago → moderate boost (gap in months)
  • Names are fuzzy-matched and respect your confirmed merges
